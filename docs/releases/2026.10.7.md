# 2026.10.7

_Released 2026-10-15 · [compare 2026.10.3…2026.10.7](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/compare/2026.10.3...2026.10.7) · [all releases](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/tree/main/docs/releases)_

The adaptive heating curve now learns from sunny days and heating periods more accurately, and shows how well it matches the pump and how closely room temperatures follow their targets. New information shows how quickly rooms cool, how much heat the home stores, and which curve adjustments have limited evidence or extend beyond observed weather. Room readings now use and record the sensor the pump actually uses; curve learning avoids non-heating hours, begins its first adjustments sooner, and returns the pump to its automatic curve when the integration is removed. Solar calibration retries sooner after a data-fetch failure, heating and hot-water efficiency readings remain available after restarts and counter resets, and sensor identities stay stable.

## More reliable adaptive-curve readiness

The readiness check now compares the learned baseline during heating periods and accounts for room compensation, so unrelated fluctuations are less likely to prevent it from passing. You can also see how closely rooms track their target, and room-compensation corrections are applied only when they match the sensor and curve-active period they belong to.

_Related: [#342](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/342), [#343](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/343), [#350](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/350), [#354](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/354), [#363](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/363), [#364](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/364)_

## Better indoor temperature history

External room temperatures are now recorded in both cloud and Modbus modes. If a selected indoor sensor has not yet built up enough history, the integration can use another available indoor reading in the meantime.

_Related: [#344](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/344), [#348](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/348), [#352](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/352)_

## More accurate and quicker curve adjustments

Curve adjustments now use heating hours, avoiding room-temperature changes during idle or hot-water periods. The integration also shows how much data supports each adjustment and can make its first adjustments sooner, while keeping the usual slower cadence afterward.

_Related: [#353](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/353), [#355](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/355), [#357](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/357)_

## Clearer curve details and release updates

Curve information now identifies outdoor-temperature points beyond the range covered by observations, and support details include more of the information needed to understand curve readiness and room tracking. Release highlights can also be added before publication so they appear in the release update.

_Related: [#338](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/338), [#356](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/356), [#359](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/359)_

## Improved solar contribution estimates

The adaptive curve can now estimate how much solar heating reduces the home's heating demand using daily energy data. If a calibration attempt cannot retrieve its inputs, it retries sooner; completed attempts keep the usual daily interval.

_Related: [#360](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/360), [#361](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/361)_

## New information about how quickly your home cools

The integration can estimate the home's thermal time constant and thermal mass from room-temperature declines when heating is off. These figures are shown only when there is enough data, and they are informational rather than changes to heating behavior.

_Related: [#358](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/358)_

## More dependable sensor readings

Sensor identities stay stable when Home Assistant changes how device information is represented. Heating and hot-water COP readings also remain visible across restarts and counter resets, with their age shown so you can tell when they were last calculated.

_Related: [#340](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/340), [#362](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/362)_

## Restore the pump's automatic curve when removing the integration

If the adaptive curve is active when you remove the integration, the pump is returned to its own automatic curve when conditions allow. Unloading or restarting Home Assistant does not trigger this change.

_Related: [#347](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/347)_

_Internal changes (refactor / tests / CI / docs), not described in detail: #336, #337, #341, #345, #346, #351._

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Setup is **Cloud HTTP** or **local Modbus**.
> Local Modbus is read-only until writing is enabled.
> SmartControl stay cloud-only.


> [!WARNING]
> **Modbus mode**
> Enabling Modbus writing means you accept full responsibility for values written to the pump.
> Incorrect or out-of-range values may void the warranty and/or affect lifecycle and performance.


> [!WARNING]
> **Cloud mode**
> Elevate access assigns "Remote Service" (service tech) rights to your user. Cloud HTTP only.
> Cloud mode uses the same internal API as the app. Use at your own risk ;)
