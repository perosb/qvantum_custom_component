# 2026.10.10

_Released 2026-10-19 · [compare 2026.10.8…2026.10.10](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/compare/2026.10.8...2026.10.10) · [all releases](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/tree/main/docs/releases)_

Forecasts now stay available after a failed update when they’re still current, and hot-water efficiency readings better reflect each heating cycle, including auxiliary heat; the standing-loss estimate also survives restarts, and the tank’s effective size is estimated. The adaptive heating curve keeps its points in order and shows which points are estimated or limited, with a notice if the pump stops following it. Heating and hot-water priority times can now be viewed and adjusted, compressor power averages are available again, and curve reverts are easier to understand.

## Forecasts stay available during brief service interruptions

A recent weather forecast is kept when a refresh temporarily fails, as long as it still covers the current time. This helps prevent weather-based features from becoming unavailable because of a brief interruption.

_Related: [#377](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/377)_

## More dependable heating and hot-water performance readings

Electrical use from auxiliary hot-water heating is now counted toward hot water rather than space heating, making the related performance readings more accurate. Compressor power is available for average reporting, and the estimate of hot-water tank heat loss is retained across Home Assistant restarts.

_Related: [#378](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/378), [#387](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/387), [#388](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/388)_

## Adaptive heating curves are clearer and more reliable

Curve details show which points are based on estimates beyond the observed outdoor temperatures and which are limited by the heat pump. Improvements help keep the curve within valid limits, and the integration now reports when the pump switches away from it or why it falls back to its automatic setting.

_Related: [#384](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/384), [#385](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/385), [#394](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/394), [#395](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/395), [#397](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/397)_

## Priority windows follow the pump’s scheduling

Heating, cooling and hot-water priority windows can be adjusted in minutes using 30-minute steps. Heating and hot-water windows have a 30-minute minimum, while cooling can be set to zero.

_Related: [#386](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/386), [#389](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/389), [#391](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/391)_

## Improved compatibility with newer Home Assistant versions

A behind-the-scenes compatibility update helps the integration continue working with newer Home Assistant versions.

_Related: [#396](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/396)_

> Local Modbus is read-only until writing is enabled.
> SmartControl stay cloud-only.


> [!WARNING]
> **Modbus mode**
> Enabling Modbus writing means you accept full responsibility for values written to the pump.
> Incorrect or out-of-range values may void the warranty and/or affect lifecycle and performance.


> [!WARNING]
> **Cloud mode**
> Elevate access assigns "Remote Service" (service tech) rights to your user. Cloud HTTP only.
> Cloud mode uses the same internal API as the app. Use at your own risk ;)
