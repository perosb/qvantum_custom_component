# 2026.9.14

_Released 2026-09-20 · [compare 2026.9.11…2026.9.14](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/compare/2026.9.11...2026.9.14) · [all releases](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/tree/main/docs/releases)_

You can now manage hot water and adjust the heating curve in either connection mode. New automation triggers and conditions cover key heat-pump states, and Diagnostics now groups status information alongside compressor and fan run times and compressor starts. During temporary service outages, existing settings are preserved and Home Assistant correctly shows the integration as unavailable.

## Cloud and local connection improvements

The integration now uses dedicated communication paths for cloud and local-network connections, selecting the one that matches your setup. This lays the groundwork for ongoing support of both connection types.

_Related: [#207](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/207), [#209](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/209), [#210](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/210), [#213](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/213)_

## More graceful handling of cloud outages

Temporary cloud server errors no longer erase cached settings. When an update cannot be completed, affected entities are marked unavailable instead of losing their settings-derived values.

_Related: [#220](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/220)_

## Simpler domestic hot water control

A water-heater control lets you manage domestic hot water temperature and choose Eco, Normal, Extra, Smart, or Off modes. Existing hot-water controls remain available too.

_Related: [#223](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/223)_

## Status alerts and equipment diagnostics

Status information is grouped under Diagnostics, and device triggers and conditions are available for events such as defrost or a ventilation filter being due soon. In local-network mode, you can also track compressor and ventilation fan operating hours and compressor starts.

_Related: [#222](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/222), [#225](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/225), [#226](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/226)_

## Heating curve controls across connection types

You can view and configure the heating curve, including choosing automatic or user-defined settings and adjusting user-defined curve points. These controls are available with both cloud and local-network connections.

_Related: [#228](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/228), [#230](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/230)_

_Internal changes (refactor / tests / CI / docs), not described in detail: #208, #211, #214, #221, #224, #227, #231._

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Setup is **Cloud HTTP** or **local Modbus**.
> Local Modbus is read-only until writing is enabled.
> SmartControl stay cloud-only.


> [!WARNING]
> **Modbus mode**
> Enabling Modbus writing means you accept full responsibility for values written to the pump.
> Incorrect or out-of-range values may void the warranty and/or affect lifecycle and performance.


> [!WARNING]
> **Cloud mode**
> Elevate access assigns "Remote Service" (service tech) rights to your user. Cloud HTTP only.
> Cloud mode uses the same internal API as the app. Use at your own risk ;)
