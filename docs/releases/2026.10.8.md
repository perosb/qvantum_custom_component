# 2026.10.8

_Released 2026-10-16 · [compare 2026.10.3…2026.10.8](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/compare/2026.10.3...2026.10.8) · [all releases](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/tree/main/docs/releases)_

The adaptive heating curve now shows more clearly whether its learning is ready, how closely room temperatures match their targets, which points rely on weather outside the measured range, and how much evidence supports each adjustment; it also better accounts for sunshine and reports how quickly the home cools and stores heat. Curve learning now uses more suitable heating periods and indoor readings, can fall back to another available reading while a new sensor builds history, and returns the pump to its automatic curve when the integration is removed. Solar learning retries sooner after temporary data problems, and heating and hot-water efficiency readings remain available across Home Assistant restarts and counter resets. You can also save or download an optional system summary to share; it leaves out identifying details and raw readings, and is never sent automatically.

Release highlights can be prepared before a release is published, so the announcement and release notes can share the same overview.

## More reliable heating-curve learning

The curve now judges readiness using relevant heating hours and accounts for room compensation. Trims ignore non-heating periods, show how much evidence supports each point, and can begin filling sooner; points outside the observed outdoor-temperature range are identified. If you remove the integration while its curve is active, it attempts to restore the pump’s own Auto curve.

## Better room-temperature tracking

External room temperatures are now recorded for cloud and Modbus systems, with another indoor sensor used temporarily if the preferred one has not yet gathered enough history. New readings show how closely the room follows its target, and provide more context about room compensation and the measurements behind it.

## Improved solar learning

The heating curve can now estimate how much solar warmth reduces heating demand using whole-day data. If required data cannot be fetched, the system retries sooner; completed attempts keep their normal schedule.

_Related: [#360](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/360), [#361](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/361)_

## Insights into how your home stores heat

New diagnostics estimate how quickly your home cools and how much heat it can store, when enough measurements are available. These are informational measurements and do not change how the heat pump operates.

_Related: [#358](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/358)_

## More visibility into curve safeguards

Diagnostics now include curve details that can help explain readiness, including points beyond the observed outdoor-temperature range and whether the indoor-temperature safeguard has a usable reading.

_Related: [#359](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/359), [#365](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/365)_

## Easier sharing of installation summaries

You can export a summary of selected device settings and measured heating characteristics, then download it while signed in or access the local file and service response. The summary is not sent anywhere automatically.

_Related: [#366](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/366), [#368](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/368)_

## More dependable sensor and COP readings

Entity identities remain stable when Home Assistant changes how device information is represented. Heating and hot-water COP readings remain visible across restarts and counter resets, with their age shown so you can tell when they were last calculated.

_Related: [#340](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/340), [#362](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/362)_

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Setup is **Cloud HTTP** or **local Modbus**.
> Local Modbus is read-only until writing is enabled.
> SmartControl stay cloud-only.


> [!WARNING]
> **Modbus mode**
> Enabling Modbus writing means you accept full responsibility for values written to the pump.
> Incorrect or out-of-range values may void the warranty and/or affect lifecycle and performance.


> [!WARNING]
> **Cloud mode**
> Elevate access assigns "Remote Service" (service tech) rights to your user. Cloud HTTP only.
> Cloud mode uses the same internal API as the app. Use at your own risk ;)
