# 2026.9.11

_Released 2026-09-10 · [compare 2026.9.3…2026.9.11](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/compare/2026.9.3...2026.9.11) · [all releases](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/tree/main/docs/releases)_

You can now adjust the outdoor-temperature limit for stopping heating in either setup, and see more pump status, connection, vacation, and relay indicators. Temperature settings now show Celsius, and climate control works with an external room sensor, shows its reading, and allows adjustments in 0.1° steps; controls also appear only in setups that support them. The update also fixes extra-hot-water timer behavior and improves how settings and device information are handled.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Setup is **Cloud HTTP** or **local Modbus**.
> Local Modbus is read-only until writing is enabled.
> SmartControl stay cloud-only.


> [!WARNING]
> **Modbus mode**
> Enabling Modbus writing means you accept full responsibility for values written to the pump.
> Incorrect or out-of-range values may void the warranty and/or affect lifecycle and performance.


> [!WARNING]
> **Cloud mode**
> Elevate access assigns "Remote Service" (service tech) rights to your user. Cloud HTTP only.
> Cloud mode uses the same internal API as the app. Use at your own risk ;)

## Temperature settings across connection modes

Outdoor temperature stop heating is now available as a setting in both cloud and local modes. Temperature settings show Celsius units and are identified as temperature controls for a clearer Home Assistant display.

_Related: [#186](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/186), [#187](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/187), [#188](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/188), [#189](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/189), [#201](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/201)_

## Clearer status and controls in local mode

Local mode now shows more heat pump, connectivity, vacation, and relay status information. Cloud-only adaptive controls are no longer shown when using a local connection.

_Related: [#191](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/191), [#196](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/196), [#203](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/203)_

## Extra hot water stop behavior

Stopping Extra hot water now cancels its pending restore timer, preventing the previous setting from being restored later.

_Related: [#192](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/192)_

## More reliable hot water settings

Hot water start and stop settings are now retrieved from the correct source, avoiding repeated missing-data messages and helping those settings update reliably.

_Related: [#197](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/197)_

## Improved device compatibility

The integration now uses supported Home Assistant device lookups, helping it remain compatible with newer Home Assistant versions and keeping device cleanup limited to the right heat pump.

_Related: [#200](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/200), [#202](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/202)_

## Climate control with an external room sensor

Climate control can now use an external room sensor for its current temperature and target-temperature controls. The climate target can also be adjusted in 0.1°C increments, and the climate card remains available when the selected external reading is present.

_Related: [#204](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/204), [#205](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/205), [#206](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/206)_
