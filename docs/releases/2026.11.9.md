# 2026.11.9

_Released 2026-11-18 · [compare 2026.11.8…2026.11.9](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/compare/2026.11.8...2026.11.9) · [all releases](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/tree/main/docs/releases)_

Added a room-temperature recommendation for the adaptive heating curve—reduce, keep, or increase—that’s available after 24 hours of readings, before the curve is ready, and shows whether it refers to the pump’s current curve or the user’s curve once active. The updated guides explain the feature’s requirements, safeguards, limits and how to stop it, as well as where to get help; diagnostic downloads are redacted before sharing. They also clarify how power readings and heating estimates work, including when estimates are held or rejected, and how efficiency comparisons use the same time period.

## Get guidance on adjusting your heating curve

A new sensor recommends whether to reduce, keep, or increase the heating curve based on at least 24 hours of room-temperature readings. It can provide guidance before the integration considers the curve ready.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Setup is **Cloud HTTP** or **local Modbus**.
> Local Modbus is read-only until writing is enabled.
> SmartControl stay cloud-only.


> [!WARNING]
> **Modbus mode**
> Enabling Modbus writing means you accept full responsibility for values written to the pump.
> Incorrect or out-of-range values may void the warranty and/or affect lifecycle and performance.


> [!WARNING]
> **Cloud mode**
> Elevate access assigns "Remote Service" (service tech) rights to your user. Cloud HTTP only.
> Cloud mode uses the same internal API as the app. Use at your own risk ;)
