# 2026.11.13

_Released 2026-11-25 · [compare 2026.11.10…2026.11.13](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/compare/2026.11.10...2026.11.13) · [all releases](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/tree/main/docs/releases)_

This update makes efficiency readings easier to understand by showing how many days of data they cover and adding a separate estimate in the EU energy label’s scale. It adds information about electricity used to heat the hot-water tank, defrosts, ventilation and heating performance, and gives reports more context for understanding those readings. It also improves heating adjustments after a target change or when the home is cold, prevents an unreliable low reading from reducing heat, and changes hot-water meter warnings to focus on meaningful changes or unusually large differences rather than a steady offset.

## Understand efficiency scores and EU energy labels

See how much data supports the 30- and 90-day efficiency figures, and compare measured performance with a derived EU energy class. Diagnostic reports now include the same label information as the efficiency summary.

_Related: [#539](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/539), [#540](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/540), [#547](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/547)_

## Make indoor temperature adjustments more responsive

A change to the target temperature clears older room readings so they do not skew the indoor adjustment. When the house is colder than the target, the adjustment now responds to the current reading rather than waiting for the daily average.

_Related: [#555](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/555)_

## Improve hot-water monitoring

Warnings now focus on a changing meter difference or an exceptionally large mismatch, rather than a steady difference that may reflect measurement bias. New counters also show electricity attributed to charging the hot-water tank, including the portion used by the heating element.

_Related: [#557](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/557), [#558](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/558)_

## Keep a lasting count of defrosts

A new counter records completed defrost requests, making it possible to see defrost activity over time even after older pump readings are no longer available.

_Related: [#560](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/560)_

## Make heating performance readings easier to interpret

Heating adjustments now ignore thermal readings that are implausibly low compared with the compressor’s electricity use. Reports also include the thermal and electrical readings, their data coverage, the air-side temperature drop, and ventilation speed and mode to help explain performance.

_Related: [#564](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/564), [#566](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/566), [#569](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/569), [#570](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/570)_
