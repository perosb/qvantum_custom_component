# 2026.10.19

_Released 2026-10-25 · [compare 2026.10.18…2026.10.19](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/compare/2026.10.18...2026.10.19) · [all releases](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/tree/main/docs/releases)_

When the pump’s start and stop temperatures don’t match a listed capacity setting, the notice now explains this, shows the temperatures and reported level, and no longer suggests choosing a level or changing the temperatures; the temperatures remain editable. The hot-water estimates are now called “Remaining shower minutes” and “Showers remaining” to better describe what they measure, and existing automations and dashboards continue to work.

## Clearer capacity guidance

Capacity guidance now explains when the heat pump’s start and stop temperatures do not match the integration’s older capacity table. It no longer suggests choosing a level or changing the temperatures.

_Related: [#441](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/441)_

## Clearer hot-water estimates

The hot-water estimates are now named for what they show: remaining shower minutes and showers remaining. Existing installations keep their entity IDs, so automations and dashboards continue to work.

_Related: [#446](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/446)_

_Internal changes (refactor / tests / CI / docs), not described in detail: #442, #443, #444, #445._

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Setup is **Cloud HTTP** or **local Modbus**.
> Local Modbus is read-only until writing is enabled.
> SmartControl stay cloud-only.


> [!WARNING]
> **Modbus mode**
> Enabling Modbus writing means you accept full responsibility for values written to the pump.
> Incorrect or out-of-range values may void the warranty and/or affect lifecycle and performance.


> [!WARNING]
> **Cloud mode**
> Elevate access assigns "Remote Service" (service tech) rights to your user. Cloud HTTP only.
> Cloud mode uses the same internal API as the app. Use at your own risk ;)
