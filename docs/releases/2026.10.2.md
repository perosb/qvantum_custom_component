# 2026.10.2

_Released 2026-10-10 · [compare 2026.9.16…2026.10.2](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/compare/2026.9.16...2026.10.2) · [all releases](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/tree/main/docs/releases)_

This release adds an adaptive heating curve that can use weather forecasts and sunlight, with optional adjustments for efficiency and approaching cold weather, plus a card to view how the curve is behaving. New insights cover heating and estimated hot-water efficiency, home heat loss, hot-water losses, compressor activity and solar gain; you can also keep the pump’s external room-temperature reading updated from a Home Assistant sensor. Fixes make readings and controls more dependable, with safer value limits, clearer controls when changes aren’t available, and better recovery from connection, data and saved-state problems. Efficiency readings are more accurate and steady, and detailed machine information is now grouped under Diagnostics.

## More reliable access and updates

Unavailable or outdated readings are handled more clearly when updates fail, and saved hot-water settings recover more safely after errors or restarts. Read-only users can still see equipment status, while controls that cannot be used are hidden.

_Related: [#274](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/274), [#275](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/275), [#277](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/277), [#278](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/278)_

## Safer settings and hot-water controls

Pump settings are checked before they are sent, and paired hot-water settings are applied together to avoid partial changes. Hot-water modes now match what the pump supports, room-temperature settings offer appropriate ranges, and an optional temperature sensor can keep the pump’s external room-temperature reading fresh.

## Adaptive heating curve

A new optional heating curve uses weather forecasts, solar gain, pump behavior and indoor conditions to suggest and, when enabled, adjust the curve. You can inspect the curve and its adjustments, and safeguards return the pump to its automatic setting if an update cannot be applied safely.

## More insight into efficiency and system performance

New readings and estimates help show heating efficiency, building heat loss, hot-water losses, compressor operation and heat-meter plausibility. These figures are based on available readings and history, and some need enough data before they appear.

_Related: [#302](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/302), [#303](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/303), [#305](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/305), [#307](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/307), [#310](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/310)_

## Current solar conditions and estimated solar gain

See smoothed solar irradiance and an estimate of solar heat gain based on local weather and the heat pump’s data. These readings may be unavailable until a location and enough history are available.

_Related: [#311](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/311)_

## Clearer sensor lists and useful readings enabled by default

Supported calculated readings are enabled by default, but may remain unavailable until enough history has accumulated. Technical readings are grouped under Diagnostics so the main list focuses on everyday temperatures, energy, comfort and efficiency.

_Related: [#312](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/312), [#313](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/313)_

## More dependable COP readings

Heating COP now uses a rolling energy window, and the hot-water COP is an estimate based on the tank’s energy balance. Recent COP readings are kept across short mode changes and restarts, while stale or unreliable values are not shown.

_Related: [#322](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/322), [#324](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/324), [#325](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/325), [#327](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/327), [#328](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/328), [#331](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/331)_

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Setup is **Cloud HTTP** or **local Modbus**.
> Local Modbus is read-only until writing is enabled.
> SmartControl stay cloud-only.


> [!WARNING]
> **Modbus mode**
> Enabling Modbus writing means you accept full responsibility for values written to the pump.
> Incorrect or out-of-range values may void the warranty and/or affect lifecycle and performance.


> [!WARNING]
> **Cloud mode**
> Elevate access assigns "Remote Service" (service tech) rights to your user. Cloud HTTP only.
> Cloud mode uses the same internal API as the app. Use at your own risk ;)
