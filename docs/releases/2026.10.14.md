# 2026.10.14

_Released 2026-10-24 · [compare 2026.10.13…2026.10.14](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/compare/2026.10.13...2026.10.14) · [all releases](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/tree/main/docs/releases)_

The integration now gathers hourly wind data and can use it to adjust the heating curve when there is enough reliable evidence; wind adjustment is off by default. Wind estimates now need enough cold, windy hours and consistent results across the data period, so unreliable estimates won’t affect the curve. Configure now lets you turn solar, COP feedback, pre-charge and wind adjustments on or off; solar is on by default and the others are off. The card now shows optional adjustments when they contribute.

## Wind readings added to weather data

The integration now collects hourly wind readings alongside solar data from the weather service. These readings do not affect the heat curve on their own.

_Related: [#428](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/428)_

## Wind impact can be estimated

The integration can estimate how wind relates to heat loss using past data and reports whether there is enough evidence for an estimate. Results are specific to each installation.

_Related: [#429](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/429)_

## Stricter checks for wind estimates

Wind estimates now require enough cold, windy hours and agreement across separate parts of the data. This helps prevent an unreliable estimate from being treated as a real wind effect.

_Related: [#431](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/431)_

## Optional wind adjustment for the heat curve

You can enable a wind adjustment that responds to forecast wind when the estimate is considered reliable. It is off by default, and missing wind readings do not count as calm conditions.

_Related: [#430](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/430)_

## Configure curve terms and see their effects

The Configure dialog now lets you switch curve adjustments on or off, including solar, wind, COP feedback and pre-charge. The curve card also shows optional adjustments when they contribute.

_Related: [#432](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/432)_

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Setup is **Cloud HTTP** or **local Modbus**.
> Local Modbus is read-only until writing is enabled.
> SmartControl stay cloud-only.


> [!WARNING]
> **Modbus mode**
> Enabling Modbus writing means you accept full responsibility for values written to the pump.
> Incorrect or out-of-range values may void the warranty and/or affect lifecycle and performance.


> [!WARNING]
> **Cloud mode**
> Elevate access assigns "Remote Service" (service tech) rights to your user. Cloud HTTP only.
> Cloud mode uses the same internal API as the app. Use at your own risk ;)
