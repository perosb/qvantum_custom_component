# 2026.10.15

_Released 2026-10-24 · [compare 2026.10.13…2026.10.15](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/compare/2026.10.13...2026.10.15) · [all releases](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/tree/main/docs/releases)_

The integration now collects hourly wind readings and can estimate wind’s effect on heating, but the wind adjustment only acts when enabled and supported by reliable evidence. A fix prevents misleading estimates by requiring enough cold, windy hours and consistent results across the period. Configure now lets you switch solar, COP feedback, pre-heating, and wind adjustments on or off; solar is on by default and the others are off, and the card distinguishes active adjustments from enabled-but-idle ones and shows when wind is identifiable even while switched off.

## Wind readings are collected for future adaptation

The integration now retrieves hourly wind readings alongside solar weather data. Missing wind readings are treated as unavailable, so they do not disrupt the existing curve.

_Related: [#428](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/428)_

## Wind estimates require stronger evidence

The integration now checks that wind has a clear, consistent effect before considering it usable. This avoids relying on misleading estimates, especially when there are too few cold, windy hours.

_Related: [#429](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/429), [#431](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/431)_

## Wind can optionally adjust the heating curve

When enabled, wind can contribute an adjustment of up to 2 °C, but only when the wind estimate is reliable. This option is off by default.

_Related: [#430](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/430)_

## Control curve adjustments in Configure

Configure now lets you switch solar, COP feedback, pre-charge and wind adjustments on or off. Solar is on by default, while the other three are off; a switch cannot make an adjustment act when its estimate is not valid.

_Related: [#432](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/432)_

## See when an adjustment is enabled but inactive

The card now distinguishes an adjustment that is switched off from one that is enabled but currently has no effect. It can also show when wind is switched off but a usable wind estimate is available.

_Related: [#433](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/433)_

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Setup is **Cloud HTTP** or **local Modbus**.
> Local Modbus is read-only until writing is enabled.
> SmartControl stay cloud-only.


> [!WARNING]
> **Modbus mode**
> Enabling Modbus writing means you accept full responsibility for values written to the pump.
> Incorrect or out-of-range values may void the warranty and/or affect lifecycle and performance.


> [!WARNING]
> **Cloud mode**
> Elevate access assigns "Remote Service" (service tech) rights to your user. Cloud HTTP only.
> Cloud mode uses the same internal API as the app. Use at your own risk ;)
