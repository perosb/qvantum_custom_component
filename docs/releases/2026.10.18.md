# 2026.10.18

_Released 2026-10-25 · [compare 2026.10.13…2026.10.18](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/compare/2026.10.13...2026.10.18) · [all releases](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/tree/main/docs/releases)_

The integration now gathers wind readings and can use them as an optional heating-curve adjustment when there is enough reliable evidence; you can manage all curve adjustments in Configure, and the card and wind status sensor show whether wind adjustment is available, enabled, or active. You can also run calibration on demand and see the updated results right away. Wind estimates now require stronger, consistent evidence and are retained across restarts, and several translated names have been corrected.

## Wind data and reliable estimates

The integration now gathers wind readings and uses them to check whether your home’s history supports a reliable estimate of wind’s effect on heating. Stricter checks help prevent weak or misleading estimates from being used.

_Related: [#428](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/428), [#429](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/429), [#431](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/431)_

## Optional wind adjustment

You can enable a wind-based adjustment to the heating curve, which is off by default and only acts when the wind estimate is reliable. The adjustment is limited and will not treat missing wind readings as calm weather.

_Related: [#430](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/430)_

## Control curve terms and see their status

The Configure screen now lets you switch each curve adjustment on or off, and the card shows when an adjustment is contributing, idle, or unavailable. Wind can also be shown as identifiable but switched off, so you can tell the difference between an inactive adjustment and one without enough evidence.

_Related: [#432](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/432), [#433](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/433)_

## Refresh and retain calibration results

Wind estimates are now retained when the integration restarts. You can also request a calibration attempt at any time, and the updated results appear immediately afterward.

_Related: [#434](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/434), [#435](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/435), [#438](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/438)_

## Wind model details and clearer naming

A dedicated diagnostic sensor shows the wind estimate, whether it is usable, and why it may not be available. Its name is now consistent with the other adaptive-curve sensors in Swedish, Finnish, and French.

_Related: [#436](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/436), [#439](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/439)_

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Setup is **Cloud HTTP** or **local Modbus**.
> Local Modbus is read-only until writing is enabled.
> SmartControl stay cloud-only.


> [!WARNING]
> **Modbus mode**
> Enabling Modbus writing means you accept full responsibility for values written to the pump.
> Incorrect or out-of-range values may void the warranty and/or affect lifecycle and performance.


> [!WARNING]
> **Cloud mode**
> Elevate access assigns "Remote Service" (service tech) rights to your user. Cloud HTTP only.
> Cloud mode uses the same internal API as the app. Use at your own risk ;)
