# 2026.10.3

_Released 2026-10-10 · [compare 2026.9.16…2026.10.3](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/compare/2026.9.16...2026.10.3) · [all releases](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/tree/main/docs/releases)_

This release adds an adaptive heating curve that can be previewed and switched on manually, plus weather and solar insights and a card to view the curve. It also adds measures of heat-pump efficiency, home heat loss, hot-water losses and compressor activity, and can keep an external room-temperature reading up to date. Readings and controls are now more reliable, with more accurate efficiency figures that stay available across mode changes and restarts, and invalid settings are prevented. Machine details are grouped under Diagnostics, while everyday temperatures and key efficiency figures remain easy to find.

## Safer, clearer controls

Read-only accounts can still see current temperatures and fan state, while controls that cannot be used are hidden. Temperature and hot-water settings now follow the supported ranges and modes, helping prevent rejected changes.

_Related: [#274](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/274), [#278](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/278), [#296](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/296), [#297](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/297), [#300](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/300)_

## More dependable communication and hot-water control

Poll failures, slow replies and unusual server responses are handled more safely, with fewer lingering requests and better recovery. Hot-water options now match the connected pump’s capabilities, and temporary rate limits are respected.

_Related: [#275](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/275), [#276](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/276), [#277](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/277), [#280](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/280)_

## Keep an external room-temperature reading up to date

You can choose a Home Assistant temperature sensor to keep the pump’s external room-temperature reading refreshed automatically. This can help prevent the pump from losing that temperature source when the room temperature stays steady.

_Related: [#299](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/299)_

## Preview and use an adaptive heating curve

A new heating-curve feature uses weather forecasts, solar conditions and pump data to calculate a curve, which you can inspect before choosing to activate it. When active, it can learn from operation and make guarded adjustments; a dashboard card and clearer curve readings help you follow its behavior.

## More insight into efficiency and home heating

New readings help you understand heat-pump efficiency, household heat loss, compressor operation, hot-water losses and solar contribution. These longer-term insights become available as enough history is collected, and supported readings are enabled by default.

_Related: [#302](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/302), [#303](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/303), [#305](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/305), [#307](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/307), [#310](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/310), [#311](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/311), [#312](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/312), [#335](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/335)_

## A tidier sensor dashboard

Machine and service readings are grouped under Diagnostics, making the main view easier to scan while keeping the readings available for history and automations.

_Related: [#313](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/313)_

## More useful, reliable COP readings

Heating and hot-water efficiency readings now use better-suited calculations and rolling data, and recent values can survive a restart. The hot-water COP is an estimate based on the tank’s energy balance; misleading short-window readings have been removed or corrected.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Setup is **Cloud HTTP** or **local Modbus**.
> Local Modbus is read-only until writing is enabled.
> SmartControl stay cloud-only.


> [!WARNING]
> **Modbus mode**
> Enabling Modbus writing means you accept full responsibility for values written to the pump.
> Incorrect or out-of-range values may void the warranty and/or affect lifecycle and performance.


> [!WARNING]
> **Cloud mode**
> Elevate access assigns "Remote Service" (service tech) rights to your user. Cloud HTTP only.
> Cloud mode uses the same internal API as the app. Use at your own risk ;)
