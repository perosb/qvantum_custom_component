# 2026.11.12

_Released 2026-11-24 · [compare 2026.11.10…2026.11.12](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/compare/2026.11.10...2026.11.12) · [all releases](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/tree/main/docs/releases)_

You can now see how many days of data support each 30- and 90-day efficiency figure, compare measured efficiency with the EU energy-label scale, and find these details in diagnostic reports. New readings track electricity used to heat the hot-water tank and completed defrosts; hot-water meter warnings now focus on significant changes, and indoor heating adjustments respond better when the target changes or rooms are colder than target. Heating-efficiency readings are also held back when too little energy has been measured, and reports now include more information to help explain readings and warnings.

## Clearer seasonal efficiency comparisons

You can now see how many days of data support the 90-day efficiency figure and compare measured performance with an EU energy-label class. Efficiency reports also include the derived label information to help explain the result.

_Related: [#539](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/539), [#540](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/540), [#547](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/547)_

## More responsive indoor temperature protection

The heating adjustment now responds to a colder-than-target home using the current room reading, while still smoothing readings on the warm side. Changing the target clears earlier readings so they don’t distort the adjustment.

_Related: [#555](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/555)_

## More useful hot-water meter warnings

Warnings now focus on a changing difference between estimated and metered hot-water use, while still flagging very large differences. The reported difference remains available for reference.

_Related: [#557](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/557)_

## Track electricity used to heat the hot-water tank

New diagnostic counters show the electricity attributed to hot-water tank charging, including the portion used by the heating element. This helps distinguish electricity used to charge the tank from hot water measured when it is drawn.

_Related: [#558](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/558)_

## Keep a long-term count of defrosts

A new counter records completed defrosts, making them visible in long-term statistics rather than relying on a temporary status that may no longer be available.

_Related: [#560](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/560)_

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Setup is **Cloud HTTP** or **local Modbus**.
> Local Modbus is read-only until writing is enabled.
> SmartControl stay cloud-only.


> [!WARNING]
> **Modbus mode**
> Enabling Modbus writing means you accept full responsibility for values written to the pump.
> Incorrect or out-of-range values may void the warranty and/or affect lifecycle and performance.


> [!WARNING]
> **Cloud mode**
> Elevate access assigns "Remote Service" (service tech) rights to your user. Cloud HTTP only.
> Cloud mode uses the same internal API as the app. Use at your own risk ;)
