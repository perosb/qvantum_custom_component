# 2026.11.14

_Released 2026-11-27 · [compare 2026.11.13…2026.11.14](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/compare/2026.11.13...2026.11.14) · [all releases](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/tree/main/docs/releases)_

This update makes heating behavior easier to understand by showing more detail about heating load, indoor limits, solar adjustments, and room-gain measurements, including when estimates were made and when a change to the pump’s room setting starts a new measurement. Solar adjustments are now calculated more appropriately from the heating system’s response, with both the requested and applied adjustments shown; trim details explain the correction currently indicated and that earlier corrections are retained, while the indoor limit is reported only when it actually changes the adjustment. It also adds information about price-list availability, readiness and efficiency, and solar-fit results when the usual fit cannot be used.

## Clearer heating adjustments and indoor limits

Heating adjustment details now show the electrical and thermal inputs behind the load estimate, along with current and average indoor margins. Indoor-limit reporting also identifies which edge was applied and avoids treating tiny rounding differences as a real adjustment.

_Related: [#571](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/571), [#575](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/575)_

## More reliable room-gain measurements

Room-gain estimates now show when they were measured, and a change to the heat pump’s room setting starts a fresh measurement period. Reports also explain when a new estimate could not be made, so an older valid result is not mistaken for a recent one.

_Related: [#572](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/572)_

## More insight into heating-curve trims

Reports now show what the latest curve fit indicates at each temperature point, separately from corrections already applied. They also clarify that existing corrections remain in place when new evidence is unavailable.

_Related: [#573](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/573)_

## More transparent solar adjustments

Solar adjustments now use the heat emitter’s response when suitable measurements are available, with a fallback when they are not. Reports show the requested adjustment before limits are applied, and can include an additional wind-aware fit for information when the main solar fit cannot be used; that extra fit does not affect control.

_Related: [#578](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/578), [#584](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/584)_

## Check SmartControl price availability

A new status shows whether the heat pump currently has a SmartControl price list available. This helps explain when smart hot-water control is unavailable.

_Related: [#576](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/576)_

## More complete installation reports

Installation reports now show whether a readiness invitation can be offered and whether it was declined. They also include the energy totals and component scores behind the efficiency figures, making those results easier to interpret.

_Related: [#577](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/577), [#583](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/583)_

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Setup is **Cloud HTTP** or **local Modbus**.
> Local Modbus is read-only until writing is enabled.
> SmartControl stay cloud-only.


> [!WARNING]
> **Modbus mode**
> Enabling Modbus writing means you accept full responsibility for values written to the pump.
> Incorrect or out-of-range values may void the warranty and/or affect lifecycle and performance.


> [!WARNING]
> **Cloud mode**
> Elevate access assigns "Remote Service" (service tech) rights to your user. Cloud HTTP only.
> Cloud mode uses the same internal API as the app. Use at your own risk ;)
