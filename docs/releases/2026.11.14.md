# 2026.11.14

_Released 2026-11-27 · [compare 2026.11.13…2026.11.14](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/compare/2026.11.13...2026.11.14) · [all releases](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/tree/main/docs/releases)_

This update makes heating behavior easier to understand by showing more detail about heating load, indoor limits, solar adjustments, and room-gain measurements, including when estimates were made and when a change to the pump’s room setting starts a new measurement. Solar adjustments are now calculated more appropriately from the heating system’s response, with both the requested and applied adjustments shown; trim details explain the correction currently indicated and that earlier corrections are retained, while the indoor limit is reported only when it actually changes the adjustment. It also adds information about price-list availability, readiness and efficiency, and solar-fit results when the usual fit cannot be used.

## More detail about heating adjustments

You can see the electrical and thermal inputs behind the load adjustment, current and average indoor margins, and the trim correction indicated by recent observations. The indoor cap is reported only when it meaningfully changes the adjustment, with the relevant limit shown.

_Related: [#571](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/571), [#573](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/573), [#575](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/575)_

## Clearer solar adjustment diagnostics

Solar compensation now uses the heat emitter’s response to estimate its effect, and shows the requested adjustment alongside the amount actually applied. When the usual solar fit is not usable, a wind-based fit can still be reported for diagnosis, but it is not applied.

_Related: [#578](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/578), [#584](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/584)_

## More trustworthy room-gain measurements

Room-gain estimates now show when they were measured, and a change to the pump’s room setting starts a fresh measurement period. Diagnostics also distinguish a recent valid estimate from an unsuccessful attempt to produce a new one.

_Related: [#572](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/572)_

## Check whether energy prices are available

A new status shows whether the heat pump currently has an energy-price list for SmartControl. The integration now fetches the information needed to make this status available.

_Related: [#576](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/576), [#586](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/586)_

## More context for readiness and efficiency

Readiness information now distinguishes whether settings can be written and whether a readiness invitation was declined, and it withholds readiness results when the pump is using a user-defined heating table without curve control. Diagnostics also include the energy counters and component scores behind the efficiency results.

_Related: [#577](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/577), [#583](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/583), [#585](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/585)_

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Setup is **Cloud HTTP** or **local Modbus**.
> Local Modbus is read-only until writing is enabled.
> SmartControl stay cloud-only.


> [!WARNING]
> **Modbus mode**
> Enabling Modbus writing means you accept full responsibility for values written to the pump.
> Incorrect or out-of-range values may void the warranty and/or affect lifecycle and performance.


> [!WARNING]
> **Cloud mode**
> Elevate access assigns "Remote Service" (service tech) rights to your user. Cloud HTTP only.
> Cloud mode uses the same internal API as the app. Use at your own risk ;)
