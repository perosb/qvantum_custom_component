# 2026.11.2

_Released 2026-11-13 · [compare 2026.11.1…2026.11.2](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/compare/2026.11.1...2026.11.2) · [all releases](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/tree/main/docs/releases)_

New readings show the pump’s filtered room temperature, heating and cooling curve offsets, calculated cooling supply temperature, and electricity-price area in the usual uppercase format. Curve summaries explain more clearly why the system is or isn’t ready, readiness is assessed only before the adaptive curve is active, and learned adjustments can be reused when it starts again. Fixes prevent an idle cooling limit from appearing as a real cooling temperature, stop heating power from being held while the compressor is off, and prevent heating efficiency from showing a value when no heat was delivered. The room-gain estimate now accounts for the heating curve’s own shape.

## More insight into room temperature and cooling

You can now see the pump’s filtered room temperature and its heating and cooling curve offsets. Cooling supply readings that reflect a heating limit rather than a real cooling target are shown as unavailable.

_Related: [#476](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/476), [#478](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/478)_

## Clearer electricity price region

The pump’s electricity price region is now available locally and displayed in the familiar uppercase format, such as SE3. The displayed value stays consistent whether it comes from the pump or the cloud.

_Related: [#477](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/477), [#479](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/479)_

## Clearer curve readiness and diagnostics

Curve diagnostics now give more context about why readiness checks pass or fail and how room-temperature deviations look. Readiness is only assessed before the curve is active, so the interface no longer shows a misleading readiness status while the curve is controlling heating.

_Related: [#481](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/481), [#484](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/484), [#486](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/486)_

## More reliable adaptive curve learning

Room-influence estimates are less affected by the shape of the heating curve, making their diagnostic results more meaningful. Recent learned corrections can also be reused when the curve is activated again, avoiding a lengthy rebuild when the heating curve still matches.

_Related: [#482](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/482), [#487](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/487)_

## More trustworthy heating power and COP readings

Heating power is no longer held at its last running value while the compressor is stopped. The heating COP also holds its last valid reading when there has not been enough delivered heat to calculate a meaningful result.

_Related: [#489](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/489), [#490](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/490)_

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Setup is **Cloud HTTP** or **local Modbus**.
> Local Modbus is read-only until writing is enabled.
> SmartControl stay cloud-only.


> [!WARNING]
> **Modbus mode**
> Enabling Modbus writing means you accept full responsibility for values written to the pump.
> Incorrect or out-of-range values may void the warranty and/or affect lifecycle and performance.


> [!WARNING]
> **Cloud mode**
> Elevate access assigns "Remote Service" (service tech) rights to your user. Cloud HTTP only.
> Cloud mode uses the same internal API as the app. Use at your own risk ;)
