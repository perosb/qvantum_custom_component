# 2026.9.3

_Released 2026-09-03 · [compare 2026.8.3…2026.9.3](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/compare/2026.8.3...2026.9.3) · [all releases](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/tree/main/docs/releases)_

You can now choose an online or local setup; the local setup works without internet, identifies the pump, and keeps online-only options out of view. Supported settings can be changed locally when writing is enabled, and the online setup adds a vacation switch. The extra hot-water timer now keeps its scheduled end time after Home Assistant restarts, and local readings are handled more reliably.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Setup is **Cloud HTTP** or **local Modbus**.
> Local Modbus is read-only until writing is enabled.
> SmartControl stay cloud-only.


> [!WARNING]
> **Modbus mode**
> Enabling Modbus writing means you accept full responsibility for values written to the pump.
> Incorrect or out-of-range values may void the warranty and/or affect lifecycle and performance.


> [!WARNING]
> **Cloud mode**
> Elevate access assigns "Remote Service" (service tech) rights to your user. Cloud HTTP only.
> Cloud mode uses the same internal API as the app. Use at your own risk ;)

## Choose cloud or local connection

Setup now lets you choose a cloud or local connection. In local mode, the integration works without contacting the cloud, and heat pump readings are collected through a more reliable connection.

_Related: [#176](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/176), [#178](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/178), [#179](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/179)_

## Read heat pump identification details

Local identification can now provide the heat pump’s serial number and firmware version.

_Related: [#177](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/177)_

## Local controls match what your heat pump supports

In local mode, cloud-only controls and firmware sensors are hidden. You can change the controls supported by your heat pump directly.

_Related: [#180](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/180), [#181](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/181)_

## Keep extra hot water scheduling after restarts

You can set the extra hot water duration from 0 to 480 minutes, and its scheduled return to Normal is preserved across Home Assistant restarts. The existing 60-minute button is unchanged.

_Related: [#183](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/183)_

## Control vacation mode in cloud mode

Cloud mode now includes a switch for the vacation setting.

_Related: [#184](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/184)_
