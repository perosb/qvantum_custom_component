# 2026.10.17

_Released 2026-10-25 · [compare 2026.10.13…2026.10.17](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/compare/2026.10.13...2026.10.17) · [all releases](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/tree/main/docs/releases)_

Highlights: The integration now gathers wind information and can use it to adjust the heating curve when there’s enough reliable history; you can control the curve adjustments in Configure and see whether they’re active, idle, or unavailable. Wind estimates now need stronger evidence, and wind information is retained after restarts so unreliable results or missing data don’t affect the curve. You can also start a calibration on demand and check its results, including why a wind adjustment isn’t available.

## Wind data improves calibration

Hourly wind readings are now included in the heat pump’s historical and forecast weather data. The integration checks whether there is enough reliable evidence to estimate wind’s effect, and retains that estimate across restarts when it is available.

_Related: [#428](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/428), [#429](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/429), [#431](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/431), [#434](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/434)_

## Optional wind adjustment

You can enable a wind-based adjustment to the heating curve. It is off by default and only takes effect when the wind estimate is reliable and a usable reading is available.

_Related: [#430](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/430)_

## Choose which curve adjustments are active

The Configure screen now lets you switch each of the four heating-curve adjustments on or off; solar is on by default, while the others are off. The card also shows adjustments when they contribute to the curve.

_Related: [#432](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/432)_

## See when wind adjustment is available

The card now distinguishes an adjustment that is enabled but currently idle from one that is switched off or lacks a reliable estimate. A new wind diagnostic sensor shows the estimate and explains when there is not enough evidence for it to be used.

_Related: [#433](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/433), [#436](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/436)_

## Run calibration on demand

You can now start a calibration attempt when you need updated results, instead of waiting for the next scheduled attempt. The response reports whether calibration succeeded and whether the wind estimate is available.

_Related: [#435](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/435)_

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Setup is **Cloud HTTP** or **local Modbus**.
> Local Modbus is read-only until writing is enabled.
> SmartControl stay cloud-only.


> [!WARNING]
> **Modbus mode**
> Enabling Modbus writing means you accept full responsibility for values written to the pump.
> Incorrect or out-of-range values may void the warranty and/or affect lifecycle and performance.


> [!WARNING]
> **Cloud mode**
> Elevate access assigns "Remote Service" (service tech) rights to your user. Cloud HTTP only.
> Cloud mode uses the same internal API as the app. Use at your own risk ;)
