# 2026.11.11

_Released 2026-11-23 · [compare 2026.11.10…2026.11.11](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/compare/2026.11.10...2026.11.11) · [all releases](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/tree/main/docs/releases)_

You can now see how many days of readings support the 90-day efficiency figure, and view an estimated EU energy-label rating based on measured seasonal efficiency alongside the existing health grade, which remains unchanged. Efficiency details in diagnostic reports now match the summaries, and hot-water capacity choices are handled consistently, with unsupported levels rejected rather than rounded.

## See how much data backs your 90-day efficiency score

The 90-day SCOP now shows how many days of data it covers, so you can tell whether it reflects a full period or only a shorter stretch of operation.

_Related: [#539](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/539)_

## Compare measured efficiency with the EU energy label

The integration now shows an EU efficiency class alongside the measured SCOP, for both the 30-day and 90-day periods. The same figures are included in diagnostic exports to help explain how the class was derived.

_Related: [#540](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/540), [#547](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/547)_
