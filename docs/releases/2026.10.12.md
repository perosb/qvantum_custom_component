# 2026.10.12

_Released 2026-10-23 · [compare 2026.10.11…2026.10.12](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/compare/2026.10.11...2026.10.12) · [all releases](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/tree/main/docs/releases)_

Highlights: Hot-water loss, energy use and tank volume are now measured more reliably, and heating-curve guidance better reflects the room temperature used by the pump and separates points that cannot be adjusted from those that need attention. Hot-water capacity changes appear sooner, while unrecognized or conflicting settings are shown as unknown or flagged instead of guessed. The summary service now rejects invalid submissions and limits request rates, and diagnostics make energy that could not be attributed easier to spot.

## More reliable and protected summaries

Incoming summaries are checked more strictly and protected against excessive request rates. Summary downloads also identify the correct heat pump more reliably, without changing the information included.

_Related: [#405](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/405), [#406](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/406)_

## More trustworthy hot-water diagnostics

Hot-water standing loss is now compared under consistent conditions, and meter-deviation estimates use more accurate measurements. The diagnostics can also work without recorder statistics.

_Related: [#408](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/408)_

## Better hot-water energy and tank-volume estimates

Element energy is attributed more reliably, and energy that cannot be assigned is surfaced rather than silently lost. Tank volume is estimated only when other heating activity is not drawing heat from the tank.

_Related: [#415](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/415), [#416](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/416)_

## Clearer heating-curve guidance

Heating-curve points that are fixed at the pump’s minimum are distinguished from points that can be adjusted, making clamp reports more useful. In external room-sensor mode, readiness and deviation calculations now better reflect the temperature the pump uses.

_Related: [#410](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/410), [#411](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/411)_

## More accurate hot-water capacity reporting

In local mode, a confirmed capacity change appears immediately. If the pump’s temperature pair does not match a known capacity, the integration reports the level as unknown and provides a warning instead of guessing.

_Related: [#420](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/420), [#422](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/422)_

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Setup is **Cloud HTTP** or **local Modbus**.
> Local Modbus is read-only until writing is enabled.
> SmartControl stay cloud-only.


> [!WARNING]
> **Modbus mode**
> Enabling Modbus writing means you accept full responsibility for values written to the pump.
> Incorrect or out-of-range values may void the warranty and/or affect lifecycle and performance.


> [!WARNING]
> **Cloud mode**
> Elevate access assigns "Remote Service" (service tech) rights to your user. Cloud HTTP only.
> Cloud mode uses the same internal API as the app. Use at your own risk ;)
