# 2026.10.16

_Released 2026-10-25 · [compare 2026.10.13…2026.10.16](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/compare/2026.10.13...2026.10.16) · [all releases](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/tree/main/docs/releases)_

The integration now gathers wind data alongside solar data and can use it for an optional heating adjustment when there is enough reliable evidence; wind is off by default, and you can now control all four adjustments in Configure. The card shows whether an adjustment is on, inactive, or unavailable. Fixes make wind identification require stronger evidence, preserve its results after a restart, and ensure missing wind readings aren’t mistaken for calm weather or allowed to interrupt the curve.

## Hourly wind data joins solar weather data

The integration now gathers hourly wind readings alongside solar data. Missing readings remain unavailable rather than being treated as calm, and this data collection alone does not change the heating curve.

_Related: [#428](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/428)_

## More reliable wind calibration

Wind calibration now requires evidence from sufficiently cold and windy hours, and the results must be consistent across parts of the data before a wind effect is accepted. The calibration details are preserved across restarts, so the result is not lost when Home Assistant reboots.

_Related: [#429](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/429), [#431](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/431), [#434](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/434)_

## Optional wind adjustment for the heating curve

You can enable a wind-based adjustment that accounts for additional heat loss and can raise the target temperature. It is off by default and only applies when the calibration has enough evidence; missing wind readings do not count as calm.

_Related: [#430](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/430)_

## Choose which curve adjustments are enabled

The Configure dialog now lets you switch the solar, COP feedback, pre-charge, and wind adjustments on or off. Solar is enabled by default; the other adjustments are off by default, and a switch cannot make an unsupported adjustment take effect.

_Related: [#432](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/432)_

## See when adjustments are active or idle

The curve card now distinguishes adjustments that are switched off from ones that are enabled but currently idle or lack a usable model. It can also show when wind calibration is available even if the wind adjustment is switched off.

_Related: [#433](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/433)_

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Setup is **Cloud HTTP** or **local Modbus**.
> Local Modbus is read-only until writing is enabled.
> SmartControl stay cloud-only.


> [!WARNING]
> **Modbus mode**
> Enabling Modbus writing means you accept full responsibility for values written to the pump.
> Incorrect or out-of-range values may void the warranty and/or affect lifecycle and performance.


> [!WARNING]
> **Cloud mode**
> Elevate access assigns "Remote Service" (service tech) rights to your user. Cloud HTTP only.
> Cloud mode uses the same internal API as the app. Use at your own risk ;)
