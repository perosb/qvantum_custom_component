# 2026.10.21

_Released 2026-10-28 · [compare 2026.10.20…2026.10.21](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/compare/2026.10.20...2026.10.21) · [all releases](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/tree/main/docs/releases)_

Pump-speed readings are now correctly scaled, and hot-water draw power better reflects power while water is being used. QG-14 units can now show readings and operating status for each compressor separately. The documentation has also been expanded and corrected to explain the heat pump models and how heating, hot water, and other measurements are reported.

## More accurate pump speed readings

The GP2 pump speed now displays as a realistic percentage instead of values that could exceed 100%.

_Related: [#452](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/452)_

## Clearer hot-water draw power

Hot-water draw power now reflects the current draw more smoothly and accurately. The separate hot-water energy reading remains available.

_Related: [#455](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/455)_

## Individual compressor-unit readings

On supported QG heat pumps, you can now see readings for each compressor unit separately, helping reveal when one unit is stopped or unavailable. These readings appear only when the heat pump supports them.

_Related: [#460](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/460)_

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Setup is **Cloud HTTP** or **local Modbus**.
> Local Modbus is read-only until writing is enabled.
> SmartControl stay cloud-only.


> [!WARNING]
> **Modbus mode**
> Enabling Modbus writing means you accept full responsibility for values written to the pump.
> Incorrect or out-of-range values may void the warranty and/or affect lifecycle and performance.


> [!WARNING]
> **Cloud mode**
> Elevate access assigns "Remote Service" (service tech) rights to your user. Cloud HTTP only.
> Cloud mode uses the same internal API as the app. Use at your own risk ;)
