# 2026.11.4

_Released 2026-11-16 · [compare 2026.11.3…2026.11.4](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/compare/2026.11.3...2026.11.4) · [all releases](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/tree/main/docs/releases)_

The integration now uses the heat pump’s own total-power reading on newer firmware, and keeps its estimate when that reading isn’t available. It also handles restored history more reliably so long-term performance measures remain available after a database recovery, and prevents implausible jumps in reported heating energy from skewing power calculations.

## More reliable long-term performance calculations

Performance statistics now remain available after a recorder database is restored or merged. Discontinuities in the recorded totals are no longer mistaken for energy use.

_Related: [#498](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/498)_

## Filter implausible heating-energy spikes

Unusually large heating-energy increases are rejected when they would imply an implausible heat output. This helps prevent brief counter catch-ups from disrupting derived performance calculations.

_Related: [#500](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/500)_

## Use the heat pump’s total power reading

On firmware that provides it, the integration now uses the heat pump’s own total power reading for internal calculations. Older firmware and zero readings continue to use the existing estimate.

_Related: [#501](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/501)_

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Setup is **Cloud HTTP** or **local Modbus**.
> Local Modbus is read-only until writing is enabled.
> SmartControl stay cloud-only.


> [!WARNING]
> **Modbus mode**
> Enabling Modbus writing means you accept full responsibility for values written to the pump.
> Incorrect or out-of-range values may void the warranty and/or affect lifecycle and performance.


> [!WARNING]
> **Cloud mode**
> Elevate access assigns "Remote Service" (service tech) rights to your user. Cloud HTTP only.
> Cloud mode uses the same internal API as the app. Use at your own risk ;)
