# 2026.10.11

_Released 2026-10-19 · [compare 2026.10.10…2026.10.11](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/compare/2026.10.10...2026.10.11) · [all releases](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/tree/main/docs/releases)_

The integration now asks clearly before sharing an anonymous summary, which includes curve activity counts; if you agree, it can be sent weekly, sent on demand, or erased, and declining does not affect the adaptive curve. Summary grouping now uses the pump’s own limits, so it still works when a model name is unavailable. The release process was also changed to avoid conflicts when publishing updates.

## Choose whether to share an anonymous summary

After a week of use, you’ll be asked whether you want to contribute an anonymous summary. You can decline, change your mind later, or remove your contribution; your choice does not affect the adaptive curve.

_Related: [#398](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/398)_

## Track adaptive-curve activity

The summary can include counts of curve changes and days when the curve used a limited or extended value. These counts are saved across restarts and are included only while the adaptive curve is active.

_Related: [#400](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/400)_

## Protect shared summaries

Shared summaries are checked to exclude identifying details and are stored for up to two years. A contribution can be erased using its installation-specific token.

_Related: [#401](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/401)_

## Control summary uploads

If you agree to share, the integration can upload a summary at most once a week. You can also send one immediately or erase your installation’s stored summaries.

_Related: [#402](https://github.com/perosb/qvantum_custom_component/pull/402)_

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Setup is **Cloud HTTP** or **local Modbus**.
> Local Modbus is read-only until writing is enabled.
> SmartControl stay cloud-only.


> [!WARNING]
> **Modbus mode**
> Enabling Modbus writing means you accept full responsibility for values written to the pump.
> Incorrect or out-of-range values may void the warranty and/or affect lifecycle and performance.


> [!WARNING]
> **Cloud mode**
> Elevate access assigns "Remote Service" (service tech) rights to your user. Cloud HTTP only.
> Cloud mode uses the same internal API as the app. Use at your own risk ;)
